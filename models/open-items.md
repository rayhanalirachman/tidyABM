What the grammar still cannot say. Entries leave this page when they are closed
rather than staying on it struck through: the history of what closed, and of
what closing it showed, is in
[what each stress test changed](what-changed.md).

Nothing. The one entry the page carried -- state that belongs to a pair of
agents, which had to be mirrored into list columns kept aligned by hand -- is
closed by relations: `abm_relation()`, `.R` and `R_v` in every pair view, `R_v
~ expr` under a pairing, `abm_link(via =)`, `abm_pairs()`. What closing it took,
and what was decided on the way, is in
[what the Lengnick round changed](what-changed.md#what-the-lengnick-round-changed).

That is a claim about the fifty-six models in this corpus, one model outside it,
and nothing wider. The next thing the grammar cannot say will arrive with the
next model that wants it, which is how every entry that has ever been on this
page got here.
